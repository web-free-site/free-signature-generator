How to make a transparent signature for documents

A transparent PNG lets a handwritten or typed signature sit on a document without a white box around it. You can create that file, and optionally place it on a PDF, in Free Signature Generator. Processing stays on your device.

Steps

  1. Create the signature. Draw or type your name in the tool. If you are signing a formal letter, a simple dark stroke on a transparent background is usually easier to read than a faint or multicolored one.
  2. Export a transparent PNG. Download the PNG and open it to confirm the background is transparent.
  3. Place it on a PDF if needed. Open the PDF in the same tool, click the page, and move or resize the signature. Download the signed PDF and check every page.
  4. Use the PNG in other documents. You can also insert the same PNG into a word-processor letter or a form that accepts images. Do not flatten it onto a document you are not allowed to alter.

Legal limits

A visual signature on a PDF is not automatically a qualified or certificate-based electronic signature. If a court, bank, or government process requires a specific signing method, use that method.

Keep the source file

Saving the PNG means you can reuse a consistent signature instead of drawing a new one for every form.
