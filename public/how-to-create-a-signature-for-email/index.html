How to create a signature for email

An email signature can mean two things: the contact block under your messages, or a handwritten-style name image you drop into a letter. This guide covers the image. Free Signature Generator can draw or type the name and export a transparent PNG, without uploading it.

Steps

  1. Draw or type the name. Open Free Signature Generator. Draw with a mouse, trackpad, or finger, or type the name and pick a script-style font if you prefer a typed look.
  2. Download a transparent PNG. Export the signature as a PNG so the background stays clear when you place it on a white or colored email.
  3. Insert the image in your email. In your email app, attach or insert the PNG into the message, or add it to the personal signature settings if the app allows images. Resize it so it is readable but not larger than the rest of the message.
  4. Keep a copy of the file. Store the PNG in a place you can find later so every message uses the same image.

This is not a qualified electronic signature

A PNG in an email shows a name; it is not a certificate-based or qualified electronic signature, and it is not a substitute for dedicated signing platforms when those are required.

Size and clarity

A very large image can look unprofessional and may be clipped on mobile. If the edges look jagged, export again and resize inside the email app rather than stretching a small file.
